What Getsu does

Getsu removes keyboard clicks, fan hum, room noise and background voices from your microphone, for every app at once: Discord, Steam, Zoom, games and OBS.

Keyboard and mouse clicks

Mechanical switches and rapid clicking are quieter in the voice your friends hear.

Fans and cooling pads

Steady background roar from PC fans, laptop cooling pads and air conditioning.

Room noise and voices

Family or a TV in the next room stay out of your call while you speak.

Desk bumps and rumble

An 80 Hz filter removes low thumps from footsteps, desk knocks and 50 or 60 Hz hum.

Every app at once

Apps set to the Default microphone get the clean voice automatically.

Private by design

Audio is processed on your PC, never saved, and never sent over the network.

Three steps

Install, pick your microphone, and start.

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. It sets up the free virtual audio cable that carries your clean voice to other apps.

  2. Pick your microphone

    Open Getsu and choose your microphone in the microphone card.

  3. Click the power button

    Getsu starts cleaning your voice. Apps on the Default microphone use it automatically. Click again to stop.

Help

Common questions and fixes.

An app still hears background noise

Getsu sends clean audio to apps set to the Default microphone. An app that was locked to your physical microphone needs a change:

  • Steam: Settings > Voice > Voice Input Device: set it to Default or CABLE Output. Turn off Steam's own Noise Cancellation.
  • Discord: User Settings > Voice & Video > Input Device: set it to Default or CABLE Output. Turn off Krisp noise suppression.
  • Games: In the in-game audio or voice settings, choose Default System Device or CABLE Output.
  • Turn off that app's own voice activation threshold.
Bluetooth earbuds sound bad

When a Bluetooth headset uses its microphone, Windows switches it to call quality (mono, narrow band). That comes from Bluetooth, not from Getsu. A separate microphone keeps game audio in full quality.

Reporting a problem
  1. Right-click the Getsu icon in the tray and choose Save diagnostics.
  2. Attach the file it saves to a GitHub issue.

The file holds the log and the device list. It does not contain audio.

Windows SmartScreen says "Unknown publisher"

Getsu is open-source software and is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. The SHA-256 checksum of each installer is listed on the release page.

Command line: getsu-cli

Clean audio files in batches or scripts. It runs on its own, so it works while the Getsu window is open.

Clean one file

Saves the result as recording_cleaned.wav unless you give a path.

getsu-cli --clean recording.wav

Clean a folder

Processes every WAV, MP3, FLAC and OGG file in the folder.

getsu-cli --clean D:\recordings\podcast\

Stereo recordings

Links the left and right channels so the stereo image does not wobble.

getsu-cli --clean track.wav --stereo

Music and singing

Keeps room decay and vibrato. Skips the neural model and uses a longer sustain.

getsu-cli --clean vocals.wav --music-mode
Option What it does
--clean <path>File or folder to clean (required).
--stereoLinked-stereo gating for stereo and multi-channel files.
--music-modeSkips RNNoise and uses a longer sustain for music and singing.
--auto-levelRaises quiet speech toward -24 dBFS.
--gain <dB>Adds makeup gain after cleaning (for example --gain 3.5).
--no-rnnoiseTurns off the neural noise reduction (filter and gate only).
--no-gateTurns off the noise gate (neural noise reduction only).
--report-json <file>Writes a frame-by-frame JSON log.
--no-progressHides the progress bar, useful in scripts.
--no-statsHides the summary table.
--check-devicesPrints the audio devices and a diagnostics report.
--probe-micTries to open a microphone several ways and reports which one works. Changes no settings.

Limits

What to know before you install.

Windows only

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. There is no macOS or Linux version.

Needs a virtual audio cable

Getsu uses the free VB-CABLE driver to pass clean audio to other apps. The installer sets it up.

Unsigned installer

Windows may show an "Unknown publisher" warning. See the SmartScreen answer in Help.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from Discord's Krisp?

Krisp in Discord only cleans your voice inside Discord. Getsu cleans the microphone for every app that uses it, including Steam, Zoom, OBS Studio and in-game voice chat.

Do I need an NVIDIA GPU?

No. Getsu runs on the CPU, so it works on AMD, Intel and budget laptops without a dedicated graphics card.

Will it lower my FPS?

Getsu does not use the GPU. On a Ryzen 7 5800H laptop it uses about 2 percent of one CPU core. Test it on your own setup if you want to be sure.

Does it remove keyboard sounds and mouse clicks?

Yes, most of them. Getsu combines an 80 Hz rumble filter, the RNNoise neural network and a noise gate with a short pre-roll buffer, so keystrokes, clicks and desk thumps are much quieter without cutting off the start of your words.

Is it private?

Yes. Your audio is processed on your computer and never saved. Getsu makes no network connections and needs no account.
