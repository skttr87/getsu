Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Getsu's noise cancellation engine.

How does Getsu compare to Discord's built-in Krisp? +
Discord's built-in Krisp only works inside active Discord voice calls. Getsu operates system-wide at the Windows virtual driver level, meaning your noise-free microphone works across Steam voice chat, in-game voice (CS2, Valorant, Apex), Zoom, Google Meet, OBS Studio, and recording apps with zero monthly minute limits.
Do I need an NVIDIA RTX graphics card to run Getsu? +
No! Unlike NVIDIA Broadcast or RTX Voice which require a costly RTX GPU, Getsu runs on a lightweight recurrent neural network (Mozilla RNNoise) optimized for standard CPUs. It consumes under 2% CPU and works on any Intel, AMD, or budget laptop processor.
Can Getsu filter loud mechanical keyboards and desk fans? +
Yes. Getsu uses a multi-stage DSP pipeline: an 80Hz high-pass filter eliminates low-frequency fan and AC hum, while the RNNoise neural engine isolates your speech and silences mechanical keyboard clicks, mouse clicks, and room echo.
Will Getsu cause audio delay or drop my game FPS? +
No. Getsu processes audio in real-time with an ultra-low latency of under 1.2ms (well below the human threshold of perception). Because it runs on CPU with zero GPU VRAM overhead, it will not reduce your in-game frame rates.
Is Getsu safe, private, and offline? +
100% yes. Getsu is open-source under the MIT License. All audio processing runs entirely in-memory on your local PC. No audio recordings are ever saved, stored, or transmitted over the internet. No user account or login is required.
